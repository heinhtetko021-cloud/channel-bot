Jewellery Care

Preserve the Beauty

🧴

Store Properly

Keep pieces in individual pouches or compartments. Avoid direct contact between different gemstones and metals. သိုလှောင်ပုံကို ဂရုစိုက်ပါ။

💧

Avoid Chemicals

Remove jewelry before swimming, bathing, or applying perfume and lotions. Chemicals can damage gold and gemstones.

✨

Regular Cleaning

Gently clean with a soft cloth after each wear. For deeper cleaning, use lukewarm water with mild soap. ပုံမှန်သန့်ရှင်းပါ။

🔧

Professional Check

Visit us annually for a professional inspection and polish. We'll ensure settings remain secure and your piece stays radiant.
