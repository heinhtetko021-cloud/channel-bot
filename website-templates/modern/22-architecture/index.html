03

The Studio

◻

Architecture is the thoughtful making of space.

Founded in Myanmar, our practice operates at the intersection of contemporary design and local material culture. We believe that great architecture emerges from restraint — from understanding what to remove, not just what to add.

Every project begins with deep listening — to the site, the climate, the client's aspirations, and the cultural narratives embedded in place. Our process is iterative, rigorous, and always grounded in the physical reality of building.
